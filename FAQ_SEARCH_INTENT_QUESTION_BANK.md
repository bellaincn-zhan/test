# B2B Motorcycle FAQ Search-Intent Question Bank

This question bank turns the FAQ Center taxonomy into buyer-style FAQ questions for Google Search, AI answer engines, marketplace searches, Reddit-style discussions, and Quora-style importer questions.

The current taxonomy is broad enough as an information architecture, but many entries are topic labels. For SEO and AI discovery, final FAQ pages should use natural questions that B2B buyers actually ask before sending an inquiry, comparing suppliers, or importing motorcycles.

## Completeness Assessment

The existing FAQ Center already covers the main B2B motorcycle buying journey:

- Product selection and technical fit
- Import documents and certification
- OEM/ODM customization
- CKD, SKD, and CBU export
- Pricing, MOQ, and payment
- Samples, inspection, and factory visits
- Quality control
- Spare parts and warranty
- Packing and shipping
- Market entry, dealership, and distributor cooperation
- Country and regional import guides
- China motorcycle supplier selection

For a professional lead-generation FAQ, the biggest improvement is to convert each topic into question-led content. The most important buyer-search gaps to emphasize are:

- Total landed cost, not just factory price
- CKD vs SKD vs CBU business strategy
- Container loading quantity by packing method
- Supplier verification and factory audit
- Road-legal certification by destination market
- MOQ flexibility, trial orders, and mixed-model containers
- Spare parts ratio, warranty claim process, and local after-sales setup
- Quality concerns around Chinese motorcycles
- Exclusive distribution requirements and sales targets
- Country-specific import rules and local homologation risk
